Why self-host your compliance stack

Compliance data is among the most sensitive information an organization holds. Kompro keeps it within your perimeter.

  • You own the database and the credentials.
  • No external service sits between you and your evidence.
  • Deploy on your own Linux host, or split frontend and API across providers.
  • Harden it to your own standards, documented in the project security guide.

The organization's compliance state is the source of truth. Frameworks are just different views over it.
